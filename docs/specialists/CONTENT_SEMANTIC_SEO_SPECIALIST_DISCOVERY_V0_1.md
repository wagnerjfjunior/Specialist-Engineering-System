# SES — Content & Semantic SEO Specialist — Discovery v0.1

**Status:** DISCOVERY / REQUIREMENTS / NOT_ARCHETYPE / NOT_CERTIFIED

## Intent
Create a reusable project-agnostic specialist for search intent, semantic/entity modeling, topical coverage, information architecture, on-page optimization, internal linking, content briefs and content-side GEO / generative discovery.

## Known requirements
- live SERP and search-intent analysis;
- keyword/query/entity clustering without reducing strategy to keyword volume;
- entity relationships, topical coverage and semantic consistency;
- information architecture and internal linking;
- titles, headings, snippets and on-page evidence;
- content quality, factual clarity, source/evidence discipline and citability;
- GEO / AI-search capability: answer-ready structure, extractable factual passages, entity clarity, authoritative sourcing and ambiguity reduction;
- Search Console query/page evidence and Semrush/Ahrefs data when available;
- preserve brand/product truth from the consumer project rather than inventing claims.

## Hard boundaries
- cannot replace Technical SEO implementation proof;
- cannot claim ranking or AI citation from content quality alone;
- cannot fabricate product attributes, prices, availability, testimonials or authority;
- cannot treat generated copy as published or indexed state;
- `CONTENT_CREATED != CONTENT_PUBLISHED`;
- `SEMANTICALLY_STRONG != RANKING_GUARANTEED`;
- `GEO_OPTIMIZED != AI_CITATION_GUARANTEED`.

## Challenge questions before candidate generation
1. Which content-quality principles are universal versus vertical-specific?
2. What evidence is required to infer search intent safely?
3. How should generative-discovery proof obligations differ from classic SERP proof?
4. Where does entity/schema design hand off to Technical SEO?
5. When does conversion copy become UX/CRO rather than SEO content?
