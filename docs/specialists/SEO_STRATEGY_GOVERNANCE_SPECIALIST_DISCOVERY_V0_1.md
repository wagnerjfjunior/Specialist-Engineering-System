# SES — SEO Strategy & Governance Specialist — Discovery v0.1

**Status:** DISCOVERY / REQUIREMENTS / NOT_ARCHETYPE / NOT_CERTIFIED

## Intent
Create a reusable project-agnostic specialist for search strategy, opportunity framing, prioritization, cross-SEO governance and proof-obligation definition without concentrating execution authority from technical, content, analytics, local, authority or paid-search disciplines.

## Known requirements
- analyze market/search landscape and live SERP;
- define organic/search-discovery objectives and measurable outcomes;
- prioritize opportunities and technical/content/authority work;
- reconcile conflicting specialist recommendations;
- frame GEO / generative discovery objectives without pretending to own all implementation;
- use Semrush/Ahrefs/Google Trends/Search Console and other surfaces when available and relevant;
- preserve evidence provenance and tool honesty;
- distinguish recommendation, product decision, authorization and verified state.

## Hard boundaries
- cannot replace Technical SEO, Content & Semantic SEO, Analytics & Growth, Local SEO, Authority/Digital PR or SEM;
- cannot authorize spend, publish campaigns, change production, accept business risk or fabricate market evidence;
- `STRATEGY_RECOMMENDED != PROJECT_AUTHORIZED`;
- `TOOL_AVAILABLE != TOOL_USED`.

## Challenge questions before candidate generation
1. Which decisions are truly governance versus execution?
2. What minimum live evidence is required before prioritizing a search opportunity?
3. How should the specialist handle contradictory GSC, SERP, Semrush/Ahrefs and business evidence?
4. Which KPIs belong to this specialist versus Analytics & Growth?
5. When should GEO become a standalone specialist rather than a cross-cutting capability?

## Reference implementation candidate
MoreNumTegra may supply real-estate search-intent, catalog, landing-page and generative-discovery cases, but must not define universal specialist truth.
