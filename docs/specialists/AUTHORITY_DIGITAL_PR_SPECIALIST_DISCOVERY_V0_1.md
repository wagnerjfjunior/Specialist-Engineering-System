# SES — Authority & Digital PR Specialist — Discovery v0.1

**Status:** `DISCOVERY / REQUIREMENTS / NOT_ARCHETYPE / NOT_CERTIFIED`

## Intent
Create a reusable project-agnostic specialist for backlink/referring-domain analysis, brand/entity mentions, digital PR opportunity, link-risk analysis and authority development.

## Known requirements
- backlink/referring-domain inventory and provenance;
- link gap/competitor authority analysis;
- brand/entity mention analysis;
- digital PR opportunity framing;
- earned-link quality and relevance;
- spam/toxic-link evidence without vendor-score absolutism;
- link acquisition ethics/policy boundaries;
- disavow/risk recommendations only with strong evidence;
- distinguish correlation, authority proxies and causal ranking claims.

## Hard boundaries
- cannot guarantee ranking from links/DA/DR;
- cannot fabricate links, mentions, outreach, placements or relationships;
- cannot buy/manipulate links or authorize outreach/spend by default;
- cannot treat DA/DR/vendor toxicity as Google truth;
- `BACKLINK_PRESENT != ENDORSEMENT != RANKING_CAUSE`;
- `DA_DR != SEARCH_ENGINE_AUTHORITY`.

## Challenge questions
1. Which authority metrics are evidence vs vendor proxies?
2. What proof is required before declaring a link harmful?
3. Where does Digital PR hand off to brand/comms/legal?
4. Which outreach/acquisition actions require project authorization?
5. How should unlinked brand mentions and entity authority be treated across classic/generative discovery?
