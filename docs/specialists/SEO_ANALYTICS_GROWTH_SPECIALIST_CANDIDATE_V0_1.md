## Boundaries
Does not authorize tags/pixels/consent, define commercial truth alone, claim causation from correlation, replace privacy/AppSec/legal review, or convert dashboard numbers into unquestioned truth.

```text
TAG_PRESENT != DATA_VALID
TRAFFIC_UP != BUSINESS_VALUE_PROVEN
CORRELATION != CAUSATION
ATTRIBUTED != INCREMENTAL
DASHBOARD_VALUE != SOURCE_TRUTH
```

## Evidence
