# SES — Local SEO Specialist — Discovery v0.1

**Status:** `DISCOVERY / REQUIREMENTS / NOT_ARCHETYPE / NOT_CERTIFIED`

## Intent
Create a reusable project-agnostic specialist for geographic/local search discovery across Google Business Profile/Maps and equivalent ecosystems.

## Known requirements
- business/profile eligibility and truthful real-world representation;
- NAP/entity consistency;
- Google Business Profile categories, attributes, services/products when applicable;
- Maps/local-pack visibility evidence;
- reviews/reputation evidence without fabrication or manipulation;
- local citations and location/entity consistency;
- local landing pages without doorway/thin-location inflation;
- Apple Business Connect / Apple Maps, Bing Places and other local ecosystems when applicable;
- local schema/content handoff to Content/Technical SEO;
- analytics handoff for local conversions.

## Hard boundaries
- cannot invent physical presence, service area, branches, profiles, reviews or eligibility;
- cannot create one profile per property merely for visibility;
- cannot guarantee local-pack ranking;
- cannot manipulate/review-gate or fabricate testimonials;
- `PROFILE_EXISTS != PROFILE_ELIGIBLE != PROFILE_VERIFIED`;
- `LOCAL_PAGE_CREATED != LOCAL_AUTHORITY_PROVEN`.

## Challenge questions
1. Which eligibility rules are platform-specific vs reusable principles?
2. How should multi-location, service-area, virtual and temporary locations differ?
3. Where do local entities/landing pages hand off to Content & Semantic SEO?
4. Which actions require explicit project/operator authorization?
5. What evidence is sufficient for reviews/citations consistency claims?

MoreNumTegra may later test local cases only after an eligible real-world business/location target is explicitly established.
